Voices from the Ground

Stories of Transformation in Rural Sindh

Behind every statistic is a human life renewed. Meet the families whose dignity has been restored through your generosity.

“

"Before the solar water pump, our women and young daughters spent six exhausting hours every day pulling brackish water from 180-foot wells under the Thar desert sun. Now, clean sweet water flows right into our village courtyard. Our girls are finally attending school instead of carrying water pots."

Mai Jamna

Chachro, Tharparkar District

“

"The 2022 deluge washed away our ancestral mud house overnight. We lived on the highway embankment under plastic sheets for months. Mehran Sahara rebuilt our permanent brick home on a 4-foot raised plinth. When heavy rains returned this monsoon, our children slept warm and completely dry."

Allah Dino Solangi

Khairpur Nathan Shah, Dadu

“

"In our village, no girl had ever finished primary school because the nearest school had no roof or electricity. Mehran Sahara installed solar panels, ceiling fans, and provided free bags and books. I dream of becoming a doctor to heal mothers in our village."

Bakhtawar & Amina

Rural Qambar-Shahdadkot
